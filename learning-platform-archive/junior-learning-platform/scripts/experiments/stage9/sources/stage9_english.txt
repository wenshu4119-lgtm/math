主题：一般现在时的be动词。
In the present simple, use am with I; use is with he, she, and it; use are with you, we, and they.
Example: She is a student. The negative form is: She is not a student. To make a yes-or-no question, move is before she: Is she a student?
否定句在be动词后加not。一般疑问句把be动词移到主语前。这里仅讨论一般现在时，不讨论过去时或虚拟语气。
