主题：一般现在时的 be 动词，仅讨论 am、is、are。
In the present simple, use am with I; use is with he, she, and it; use are with you, we, and they. A singular noun normally takes is, and a plural noun normally takes are.
To make a negative sentence, put not after the be verb: I am not; he is not; she is not; it is not; you are not; we are not; they are not. Is not can be shortened to isn't, and are not can be shortened to aren't.
To make a yes-or-no question, put am, is or are before the subject. For example, a statement with subject she and verb is becomes a question starting with Is she. Do not add do or does to make this basic be-verb question.
一般现在时描述当前状态、身份或一般事实。be 动词的形式要与主语搭配。否定句把 not 放在 be 后；一般疑问句把 be 放到主语前。
这些规则不覆盖过去时、将来时、虚拟语气、完成时、被动语态或其他实义动词；不要把此处的规则推广到所有英语句型。
